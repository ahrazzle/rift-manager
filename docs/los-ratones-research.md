# Los Ratones — Competitive History Research

Researched 2026-10-10 for the Rift Manager "Los Ratones Reality" / "Los Ratones Dream"
scenarios. All results below are real and sourced; confidence notes are inline.
Anything marked **[FANTASY]** is invented for the Dream scenario and is not real.

---

## 1. Formation

- **Founded:** announced **15 November 2024** by Marc **"Caedrel"** Lamont (British former
  pro player turned streamer/caster). The name came from a Teamfight Manager video on
  his CaedrelPlays YouTube channel (Sept 2023).
- **Concept:** a streamer-first pro team; scrims, meetings and matches streamed publicly.
  Caedrel served as owner + head coach, Molecule as analyst.
- **Initial roster** (unchanged for the entire project, Nov 2024 – Feb 2026):
  - Top: Simon "Baus" / "Thebausffs" Hofverberg (Sweden)
  - Jungle: Veljko "Velja" Čamdžić (Serbia)
  - Mid: Tim "Nemesis" Lipovšek (Slovenia) — ex-Fnatic, 2× Worlds attendee
  - Bot: Juš "Crownie" Marušič (Slovenia)
  - Support: Carl Martin Erik "Rekkles" Larsson (Sweden) — ex-T1 Esports Academy (LCK CL 2024)

## 3. Roster changes

**Players: none.** The starting five (Baus, Velja, Nemesis, Crownie, Rekkles) never changed
from announcement to disbandment. The only in-game stand-in was Caedrel at support during
NNO Cup Season 2 (Rekkles unavailable).

**Staff changes:**
- 2025: Caedrel (head coach), Andrei "Odoamne" Pascu (assistant coach), Molecule (analyst).
- Nov 2025: Odoamne and Molecule departed.
- 2026 (LEC Versus): Jakob "YamatoCannon" Mebdi (head coach), Caedrel (assistant coach),
  Kevin "Shaves" Tolman (assistant coach).

## 5. Reality scenario — suggested campaign nodes (9)

Each node = one key series, in order:
1. **"The Stream Team"** — NNO Cup S2 run (Dec 2024): Caedrel fills in at support; undefeated title vs 5 Pigs 1 Team.
2. **"Northern Kings"** — NLC Winter final vs NORD Esports (Mar 2025, Nottingham).
3. **"First of the UK"** — EMEA Masters Winter final vs Ici Japon Corp (Mar 2025).
4. **"The First Loss"** — NLC Spring: stunned by NORD in the upper semifinal, then the lower-bracket revenge final (Jun 2025, Copenhagen).
5. **"Revenge, Served Cold"** — EMEA Masters Spring final vs Barça eSports (Jun 2025), after the 0–2 group defeat.
6. **"The Three-Peat"** — NLC Summer final vs Verdant (Aug 2025, Stockholm).
7. **"The Blue Wall"** — EMEA Masters Summer semifinal vs Karmine Corp Blue (Oct 2025): the 0–3 demolition, first elimination. (Designed as a losable-but-historic node.)
8. **"0–4"** — LEC Versus opening weeks (Jan 2026): the brutal Tier-1 welcome, then the turnaround wins vs Movistar KOI and G2.
9. **"One Game Away"** — LEC Versus final day vs Team Vitality (Feb 2026): win-and-in; the loss and the tiebreak heartbreak end the run.

## 6. Dream scenario — fantasy bracket **[FANTASY — not real]**

Premise: instead of joining LEC Versus in 2026, Los Ratones earn a full LEC slot for the
**2025 season** and live up to the fairytale. Built on the real 2025 landscape —
LEC Winter: Karmine Corp; Spring: Movistar KOI; Summer: G2 (all beat G2 in finals);
Worlds 2025 (Chengdu): **T1** beat KT Rolster 3–2 for a three-peat; LEC seeds were
G2, Movistar KOI, Fnatic.

Suggested fantasy path:
- **LEC Summer playoffs:** upper-round vs **Fnatic** (W 3–1) → upper final vs **Movistar KOI**
  (W 3–2, revenge for the real EMEA Masters group loss... here it's LEC) → grand final vs
  **G2 Esports** (W 3–2) — dethroning the kings of Europe, first LEC title.
- **Worlds 2025 (Chengdu):** Swiss stage 3–1 (win vs **Bilibili Gaming**, loss vs **Gen.G**,
  wins vs **KT Rolster** and **Anyone's Legend**) → Quarterfinal vs **Hanwha Life Esports**
  (W 3–2) → Semifinal vs **Gen.G** (W 3–1, revenge) → **Grand Final vs T1 (W 3–2)** —
  ending the three-peat in game 5, Faker vs Rekkles/Nemesis storyline.

All Dream opponents are real 2025 contenders; only the outcomes are fantasy.
